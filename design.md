## Brand & Visual System Overview

- **Product**: Career GPS™ (Personal AI Job Search Operating System)
- **Design Inspiration**: Referso Modern SaaS Aesthetic (Electric Indigo `#6366F1`, Vivid Violet `#8B5CF6`, Neon Emerald `#10B981` on Deep Obsidian `#08090D`)
- **Typography**: Poppins (Headings) + Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & UI)
- **Component Architecture**: 100% React 19 + Vite (Zero legacy HTML/JS dependencies)

### Color Tokens
- **Background Canvas**: `#08090D` (Obsidian cosmic background)
- **Card Surface**: `rgba(15, 17, 26, 0.75)` (Glassmorphism with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`)
- **Card Borders**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / Active: `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`
- **Primary Accent**: `#6366F1` (Referso Royal Indigo)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#8B5CF6` (Vivid Violet)
- **Growth / ATS 96%**: `#10B981` (Neon Emerald)
- **LinkedIn / Info**: `#38BDF8` (Sky Blue)
- **Cost of Delay Alert**: `#F43F5E` (Crimson Pastel)
